How it works

1

Create a task — or get guide through Chat

Type what you need done, or chat with the AI agent to shape the right task. Whether you have a clear objective or just a rough idea, the agent meets you where you are.

2

Move it to Plan or Working — the agent takes over

Drag your task into Plan or Working and the AI agent handles the rest. It plans, executes, and delivers outputs your team can use right away.

AI Agent starts with planning

Working is easy with a solid plan. Check what the agent will do before actual working.

Schedule repeated tasks

Daily, weekly, or monthly — set it and forget it. The agent automatically moves tasks to Working when it's time, so you never have to worry about recurring work again.

Outputs ready to use right away

Each task generate outputs such as markdown file, csv, slide or image you and your team can use immediately.

Built for teams, not just solo

Initiate tasks, review plan and work, share skills and keep everyone aligned. HeyHeyHey works with your whole team, not just one person.

Chat help, always on standby

Ask questions, give direction, or check in at any point. The agent listens and adjusts without losing its place.
