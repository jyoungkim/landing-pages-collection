Two focused research engines

Start broad with marketing research, then zoom into what matters most: category-level content signals and competitor-level campaign intelligence.

1) Category Research

Submit a keyword like body lotion, electric vacuum, or toothpaste. SignalScout finds related YouTube videos, then uses LLM analysis to extract recurring keywords, messages, trends, tensions, and customer language patterns.

2) Competitor Analysis

Submit one or more competitors. SignalScout tracks current moves and campaigns, then maps public reactions so you can see what people actually think and where your positioning can win.
